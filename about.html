About the shortlist

Fewer, better recommendations.

We got tired of "top 25" lists that never actually pick anything.

The Shortlist started as a personal notes doc — things we bought, used for a few weeks, and would genuinely buy again. Eventually the list got long enough that it felt selfish to keep it to ourselves, so we turned it into this site.

Every product here earns its spot the same way: someone tries it, compares it to the obvious alternatives, and only adds it if it would survive being recommended to a friend. When you buy through one of our links, we may earn a small commission — it costs you nothing extra, and it's what keeps the list free and ad-free.

No sponsored placements. If it's on the list, it's because we'd buy it again, not because a brand paid for the spot.

What we care about

Three rules for the list

01

Tried, not skimmed

We don't list anything based on a spec sheet alone — it has to be lived with first.

02

Short over exhaustive

One good answer beats twenty options. If we can't pick a favorite, it's not ready to post.

03

Honest about the link

Every affiliate link is labeled. Commission never decides what makes the list.
