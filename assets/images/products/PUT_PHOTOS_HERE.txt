Drop your product photos in this folder.

Match the filename to the "image" field you set in
assets/js/products-data.js for each product, e.g.:

  assets/images/products/kettle.jpg

Recommended: ~800x600px (4:3), JPG or WebP, under 300KB.

Until a real photo is added, the product card will show a small
placeholder label instead of a broken image.
